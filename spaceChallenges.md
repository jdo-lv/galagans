Astronauts face several major health problems because the human body evolved to live with gravity, Earth’s atmosphere, natural light, and immediate medical help—not in space.

### **What is already available versus still experimental**

Already being used: regular exercise, carefully planned food, vitamin D, spacecraft shielding, radiation monitoring, sleep scheduling, psychological support, eye examinations, heart monitoring, and rehabilitation after landing.  
Still being researched: artificial gravity, better radiation-protection drugs, treatments for space-related eye problems, probiotics, personalized medicine based on genetics, and treatments aimed at protecting cells and mitochondria.

Bottom line: Space medicine currently relies on many protections working together rather than on one “space medicine” pill. The most difficult unsolved problems are radiation, eye changes, immune-system disruption, bone loss, and providing advanced medical care when the crew is far from Earth.
