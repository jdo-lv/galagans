Why it fits a crew in flight

Designed for flight, not for Earth

Your normal, not Earth’s

In flight, blood pressure runs 8–10 mmHg lower and core temperature ~1 °C higher. Rules compare you with your own baseline.

NASA’s alert language

Six levels mirror NASA caution and warning. Quiet when nominal; colour only for alerts, always with an icon and a word.

Reporting is a win

Streaks count check-ins, never health values. A logged symptom never breaks a streak, because astronauts under-report.

Hands-free and local

Voice briefing and voice check-in run on a local speech and language model. No cloud, and the rules still decide.
