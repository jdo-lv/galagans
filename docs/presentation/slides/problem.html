Crew Health Companion

[0:10–0:35] On the way to Mars, a message to Earth takes up to 22 minutes, one way, and sometimes there’s no link at all. So the crew has to spot health changes themselves, and many of them, like bone loss, give no early symptoms. NASA’s own standard asks for monitoring and trending on board. The app flying today, EveryWear, logs data, but it doesn’t interpret it or tell the crew what to do.
