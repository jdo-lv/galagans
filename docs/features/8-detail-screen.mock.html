Explanation

What
SpO₂ is below 94 %.
Why it matters
Low oxygen saturation can mean hypoxia from a cabin atmosphere fault, fire or smoke.
How decided
Rule SPO-01: SpO₂ < 94 % → caution. Earth clinical convention, not a NASA in-flight limit (health-indicators §4).
Source
research/health-indicators.md §4 rule · SPO-01

What next

Re-measure: warm your hands, sit still for 1 minute, finger fully in, hand at heart level. Still below 94 % → tell the CMO now.

Who to involve

  1. Self-care
  2. CMO
  3. Medical conference
  4. Urgent ground
