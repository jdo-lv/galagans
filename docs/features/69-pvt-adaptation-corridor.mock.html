Explanation

What
Your PVT median is 312 ms, 18 % slower than expected for flight day 142 (265 ms). The limit is 304.75 ms.
Why it matters
PVT slows early in flight in ISS crews, and nights of 6 h or less of sleep slow it further. Slower attention raises the risk of performance errors.
How decided
Rule COG-01: PVT median > 304.75 ms (expected 265 ms for flight day 142 + 15 %) → level 3, check. Expected = pre-flight baseline 265 ms, + 9 ms on flight days 1–60 for early-flight slowing (Dev et al. 2024, ∆z 0.5 converted to ms). The baseline is a synthetic pre-loaded profile; +15 % and the flight-day 1–60 window are team heuristics, not NASA limits.
